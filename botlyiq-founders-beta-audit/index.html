Verdict

Don't pay the $10,000.

The offer is 90 days of Slack and Zoom coaching to build agents in Grok Bot, which you already run with more than 40 bots. It comes with 34 skills (about 23,500 words in total) and early access to a dashboard that was a static mock page on stage. It sells no publishing capacity, nothing that raises volume, and no guarantee on output, so it does nothing for 300 pieces a day. The stage claims contradict each other and the seller's own page: "100% autonomous" sits beside daily yes/no approval queues, "7 days" becomes 90, and $35k a month "in question" becomes "removed". The refund depends on conditions the seller judges. Anik and his old company Lurn are under a 2023 FTC order over unsubstantiated earnings claims.

Seller track record

In September 2023 the FTC sued Lurn, Inc. and Anik Singal for unsubstantiated earnings claims in coaching programs. The stipulated order entered a $14,077,121 judgment, with $2.5 million paid and the rest suspended. It permanently bars earnings claims unless they are substantiated, and it requires the written substantiation to be available to purchasers on request. In June 2024 the FTC sent refunds to 1,922 buyers. Whether a cost-savings guarantee counts as an "earnings claim" under that order is a legal question I haven't verified. Even so, you can ask in writing for the substantiation behind the "$35k removed" and "$10k a month" figures before paying anything.

Not found: a public refund policy specific to BotlyIQ, any independent BotlyIQ reviews, any customer results. The product went public on Nov 12, 2026. UgenticAI's refund policy page covers UgenticAI programs, not Expert Scale's BotlyIQ seat.

Build plan: 300 a day inside Grok Bot

The gap is flow, not render capacity.

Intake, agent acceptance and auto-fix, publishing restart, and routing across idle subscription lanes are what get you to 300. Cut each long-form source into many units, and send generated renders down the lanes you already pay for. When you need more capacity, add monthly subscription accounts. Never buy credits or API usage.

Native text and threads (T08, Copy Humanizer)120
Short clips cut from your long-form (Clip Bot, T06)90
Carousels and stills from templates (T07, Stills and Clips Desk)60
Generated image and video (browser lanes you already have)30

This mix is a planning assumption, not measured data. The per-platform split depends on your account list.

  1. 1. Name the four gaps and set the account caps

    The work is intake, agent acceptance and auto-fix, publishing restart, and routing across idle lanes. Build a table of owned accounts by platform with a daily cap for each, and replace the flat "3 or fewer per account per day" rule with those caps. Owners: O02 ContentOS Quota.

  2. 2. Daily source quota

    Feed 3 to 5 raw inputs every day: your recordings, webinars, Content Sentry creator packets, and T03 X signals. Without source, volume becomes slop. Owners: Content Sentry Lens Desk, T03, T04.

  3. 3. Cut sources into units and route them across idle lanes

    Clip Bot and T06 make clips. T07 makes carousels. T08 writes text. Stills and Clips Desk makes stills. Each source should yield 60 to 80 units across formats. Route each render by format to an existing monthly subscription lane, each in its own browser profile with its 1Password login: two grok.com Imagine accounts (open the nearly unused one), HeyGen unlimited, VEED, Descript with Underlord, InVideo, HyperFrames, VideoClaw, Remotion, three ChatGPT Pro accounts for images, and Claude.ai. When you need more capacity, add monthly subscription accounts. Never buy credits or API usage.

  4. 4. Agent acceptance and auto-fix

    QA is agent-only. Agents use Argon 4 to watch each film and send edit instructions to the video's owner; the video agent can run that analysis itself in Argon 4 in the browser or the Gemini/Antigravity app. Score hook, claim truth, brand, spelling, and phone width with the existing floor_qa and lint scripts. A trimmed silent ending is a pass. First action: trim CH20R3 from 66.49s onward, re-run QA, and publish. Copy Humanizer reviews text. No human review.

  5. 5. Restart publishing and count

    Turn ContentOS back on. It was likely stopped over ugly code-generated images; make images in the browser with ChatGPT, Grok, or Higgsfield, and ban code-generated images. Any seat can post through Zernio, Composio, or iPhone mirroring on all three Macs. T11 publishes. O03 repairs failures. O07 reconciles a daily published count against 300 per lane, posted to one Slack line every evening.

  6. 6. Nightly self-audit routine

    At 3 a.m. it reads the day's corrections and QA failures across content bots and proposes skill diffs. Wording fixes apply automatically with a logged before and after. Changes to rules or authority wait in the queue.

  7. 7. One exception queue, not a review queue

    Films that pass agent QA ship with no human look. Only items outside the standing limits land in a queue: a new account or a new claim type. Everything inside the limits ships without asking.

  8. 8. Unpause and ramp

    Turn on the paused routines. Ramp to 50 a day in week 1, 100 in week 2, 200 in week 3, and 300 in week 4. Hold a step if the QA pass rate drops below 90% or engagement per post drops more than 25% against the prior week.
